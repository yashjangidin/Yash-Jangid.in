About

My Story

Hi, I'm Yash — I build both digital and physical things, and I don't see much of a line between the two.

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at JECRC College, which is where the technical side comes from: embedded systems, C/C++/Python/Java, data structures and algorithms, and just generally figuring out how things work under the hood.

At the same time, I run a wooden furniture manufacturing business — real tools, real materials, real deadlines. It's taught me a kind of craftsmanship and problem-solving that doesn't show up in a textbook, and it shapes how I approach every other project I take on.

What I Do

I work on websites, UI/UX design, development, branding, and digital experiences. I also build and experiment with AI-powered tools and custom digital solutions depending on what the project needs — for individuals, startups, and businesses looking to build, improve, or modernize their digital presence.

I use AI where it genuinely improves the workflow — research, ideation, design, development, automation, content — as a tool to build better and faster, not just for the sake of it.

Also Building

Outside client and coursework, I make short-form animated content for YouTube — aimed at kids and spiritual seekers — using Adobe Express. It's a completely different muscle from engineering or furniture-making, and I like it that way.
